Magnet Metro (“the game”) is developed by REPLACE WITH DEVELOPER NAME (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the iOS app, the Android app, and the browser version hosted on this site.

The short version

We do not ask for your name, your email address, or any account. We cannot identify you. Your game progress is stored on your own device and is never uploaded to us. The game shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and AdMob collects data of its own — that is the main privacy consideration here, and it is described in full below.

What the game itself stores on your device

The following is written to your device’s local storage (browser localStorage on the web, and the operating system’s standard preference store on iOS and Android). None of it is transmitted to us, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear your browser data.

WhatWhy
Scrap, lifetime scrap, upgrades, owned and equipped editions, levels completed, daily best score, daily streak, contract progress So your progress survives closing the game
A log of your most recent runs (scores and statistics)To show your personal best and history
Your sound on/off preferenceSo the game remembers it
Session counters and the date you first opened the gameTo count sessions for the anonymous statistics below
An install identifier: a random value generated on your device the first time the game runs To group anonymous statistics from a single installation. It is randomly generated, is not derived from anything about you or your device, and is not the device’s advertising identifier

Anonymous gameplay statistics

The game records events describing how it is being played — for example that a run started or ended, the score and distance reached, which level was attempted and how many times, when an ad was offered and whether it was watched, and when in-game currency was earned or spent. These carry no personal information and are linked only to the random install identifier described above.

We use these to understand where the game is too hard, which parts people enjoy, and whether people come back — nothing else. They are not used to build a profile of you, and they are not sold.

Where these are sent depends on the build: they may be sent to Google Firebase Analytics or to a server we operate, and in some builds they are not transmitted at all. If they are sent to Firebase, Google’s handling of that data is covered by the Firebase privacy documentation and the Google Privacy Policy linked below.

Advertising

The game is free and is funded by advertising supplied by Google AdMob. There are three placements: two optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit, and an occasional full-screen ad shown between runs. You are never required to watch an ad to keep playing.

To serve and measure advertising, and to guard against ad fraud, the AdMob SDK collects data independently of us. This typically includes your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, coarse (non-precise) location derived from it, device and operating system information, and records of the ads you were shown and interacted with. We do not receive this data; we see only aggregate earnings reports. Google’s use of it is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are shown a consent form before any advertising SDK starts. You can reopen it at any time from Settings → Privacy choices inside the game and change your answer.
  • On iOS, you are asked whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, ads become less relevant and nothing else changes. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Deleting everything: uninstalling the app removes all stored data, including the install identifier. You can also erase your progress at any time from Settings → Reset all progress inside the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the advertising is configured with a content rating suitable for a general teen and adult audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will act on it — though as described above, we hold nothing that identifies anyone.

Data retention and your rights

Because we hold no information that identifies you, we cannot look up, export, or delete “your” data on request — there is nothing to look it up by. You can remove everything held on your device by uninstalling the game or using the in-game reset. For data held by Google as an independent controller, use the Google links above.

Depending on where you live you may have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA or similar laws. Where those rights apply to data processed by Google in connection with advertising, exercise them through the consent form in the game and through Google’s own controls.
