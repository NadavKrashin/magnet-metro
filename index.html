How to play

The whole rule
  • Drag anywhere to fly the drone. You never touch the drone itself.
  • Tap anywhere to change which colour you are.
  • Your colour flies to you. The other colour ignores you completely.
  • Fly into your own colour and you swallow it — hazards included.
  • Fly into the other colour and it costs a cell.
On the screen
Scrap
The metal you pull in — the big number, top left. It is your score for the run and your money: everything you haul is banked and spent in the Workshop.
Multiplier
The x1 beside the score. Collect without a break and it climbs; take a hit and it resets. Most of a big score is the multiplier, not the pieces.
Cells
The pips, top right. Your lives — three to start. One goes each time you touch the wrong colour. At zero the run ends.
Magnet
The ring drawn around the drone. Anything of your colour that enters it gets pulled in. A wider magnet is the first upgrade worth owning.
Charge
The shape at the bottom: a blue circle or a red diamond, showing which colour you currently are. Pieces on the course are printed in the same two shapes.
The bar
How far through the course you are. In a Free Run there is no finish line, so it measures you against your own furthest distance instead.
On the course
Mines
Spiked pieces. Match their colour and you eat them for a large bonus; mismatch and they cost a cell.
Gates
A full-width row with no gap, in one colour. You cannot steer around a gate — you have to be the right colour. Getting one wrong never costs a cell, but it wipes your multiplier and scatters part of what you are carrying.
The Press
The wall at the end of a course: four solid rows of one colour, telegraphed well ahead. Match it and you swallow the lot — the biggest single payout in the game.
Where the scrap goes
Workshop
Where scrap is spent: a wider magnet, more cells, sharper steering, a faster multiplier, more value per piece. Upgrades apply to Free Run only.
Contracts
Standing jobs on the menu — "swallow 8 mines", and so on. They pay out on their own and replace themselves when finished.
Editions
Palette swaps — different paper, different inks. They are earned by clearing particular levels and can never be bought.
Seals
Awarded for clearing an entire world of levels. Also not for sale.
The three modes
Free Run
No finish line. It speeds up until it kills you, and the only target is your own furthest distance. This is the mode your upgrades are for.
Levels
Twenty-four fixed courses in four worlds, each with one named job. Every one is flown on a stock drone — upgrades do nothing here, so clearing a level means the same thing for everyone.
Today's Run
One course a day, identical for every player. Turn up on consecutive days and the streak bonus grows.
Sharing a run
Course code
Every course is built from a short code, so the same code always builds the same course. Share yours and a friend can play the exact run you played — paste one into Settings to take somebody else's.
